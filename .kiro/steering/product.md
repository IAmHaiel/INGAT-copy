# Product Direction — INGAT

## Vision
INGAT empowers Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) to protect their hard-earned remittances by allocating funds directly into purpose-bound smart contract accounts on Stellar. This removes family tension around budget enforcement and automates locked savings goals.

## Core Features
1. **Remittance Split**: Senders control what percentage of money goes to daily spending vs locked savings.
2. **Lock Mechanism**: The locked balance is completely inaccessible until the lock date passes.
3. **No Intermediate Backend**: Purely on-chain state verification ensuring 100% transparency and absolute ownership.
